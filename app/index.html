Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution Architecture

Transparency and diagnostic trust are paramount in precision agronomy. Our framework utilizes Swin Transformer Shifted-Window Attention Rollout paired with Grad-CAM++ to reveal the exact lesion morphology that informs model predictions.

1. Shifted Window Attention Rollout

Unlike standard Vision Transformers with global quadratic attention, Swin-B restricts multi-head self-attention to local 7×7 windows with cyclic shifts between consecutive stages. Attention Rollout aggregates window attention weights across all 24 layers to trace receptive field influence from input pixels to final 1024-dimensional embeddings.

2. Resolving Leaf Ambiguity

In Phase 2, Leaf ResNet50 suffered severe confusion between Alternaria (F1 = 0.55) and Bacterial Blight (F1 = 0.57). The Swin-B cross-attention attribution map demonstrates that Swin-B captures fine chlorotic rings and water-soaked oily borders that standard CNN receptive fields conflate.

3. Fruit Diagnostic Anchoring

Fruit lesions possess high visual contrast against healthy rind. When leaf symptoms are ambiguous, the cross-attention fusion mechanism assigns higher attention weights to the fruit branch, preventing false positive alarms in early disease stages.
